The Battle of Adwa (1896) was Ethiopia's decisive victory against Italian colonial forces. It secured Ethiopia's sovereignty during the Scramble for Africa. Key figures: Emperor Menelik II, Empress Taytu Betul. Lasted less than 2 hours. Symbolized African resistance globally. Requires historian review for any claims in game content.

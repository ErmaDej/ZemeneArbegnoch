Zemene Arbegnoch is a Telegram Mini App game themed around Ethiopia's 1880s-1930s resistance history, anchored on the 1896 Battle of Adwa as shared national pride.

**Why:** The game aims to reach young, price-sensitive, Android-first Ethiopian users through Telegram's zero-install friction. The patriotic theme provides authentic identity and action hooks without needing invented lore. Monetization must comply with Ethiopia's strict virtual-asset regulations.

**How to apply:**
- Keep combat stylized and abstract (icons/formations clashing) — no gore, no realistic violence, all-ages presentation
- Group competitive "regiments" by fictional/campaign names, never by real ethnic or regional identity
- Historical figures may be referenced factually and respectfully but do not invent quotes or events attributed to real people — flag any historical claim in a code comment as "NEEDS HISTORIAN REVIEW"
- No monetary, points-for-cash, or tradeable-token features in Phase 1 — this phase is engagement-only by design
- Verify current NBE virtual-asset stance with a lawyer before launch
- Monetization path: Telegram Stars for cosmetics (safe), sponsored tournaments with airtime/data/voucher prizes (no cash handling), cash prizes via Chapa/Telebirr only after legal clearance
- Target user profile: young, price-sensitive, Android-first, Addis Ababa youth
- Key success metric: Day-7 retention and organic invite rate
- Design every screen to degrade gracefully on patchy 3G/4G and tolerate occasional outages
